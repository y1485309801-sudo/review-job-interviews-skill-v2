# Interview Scoring Rubric

## Three independent scores

### Interview execution score

Use this score to judge interviewing ability and to compare interviews.

| Dimension | Default weight |
|---|---:|
| Answer relevance and structure | 20% |
| Evidence and project ownership | 20% |
| Professional/technical correctness | 20% |
| Communication clarity and concision | 15% |
| Business and role understanding | 15% |
| Motivation, judgment, and consistency | 10% |

### Role fit score

Measure how directly the candidate’s demonstrated education, experience, skills, industry exposure, and project type match the JD. Do not treat missing preferred experience as a communication failure.

### Progression signal score

Use only when interviewer behavior is available. Consider:

- depth and specificity of follow-ups;
- explicit positive or negative statements;
- detailed discussion of the role or future work;
- availability, compensation, location, and next-step discussion;
- whether the interviewer corrected and then re-tested a critical gap.

This is not a hiring prediction. A long role explanation can be positive, neutral, or standardized.

## Role adjustment

Keep the same execution dimensions but adjust the correctness and business subcriteria:

- Engineering: coding, architecture, debugging, security, reliability.
- Algorithm/data: fundamentals, experiment design, modeling, metrics, data handling.
- Product: user/problem understanding, prioritization, solution design, metrics, stakeholder delivery.
- Operations: user and business judgment, execution, data analysis, experimentation, review.
- Design: problem framing, design rationale, craft, iteration, stakeholder communication.
- Sales/customer success: discovery, objection handling, commercial judgment, relationship skills.
- HR/general: motivation, stability, communication, self-awareness, logistics.

## Score anchors

- **9.0-10.0:** exceptional, precise, evidence-rich, role-ready, minimal meaningful gaps.
- **8.0-8.9:** strong, consistently structured and correct, only limited weaknesses.
- **7.0-7.9:** clearly competitive; several strong answers with manageable gaps.
- **6.0-6.9:** passable; relevant evidence exists, but weaknesses or inconsistency are visible.
- **5.0-5.9:** borderline; some useful evidence, but multiple important answers are weak or missing.
- **4.0-4.9:** weak; major competency gaps or frequent failure to answer.
- **Below 4.0:** severe mismatch, unreliable evidence, or pervasive inability to respond.

Do not equate “passed” with 8+ or “failed” with below 5. Hiring outcomes depend on competition, headcount, interviewer calibration, and role fit.

## Comparison rule

Compare interview execution scores, not blended totals. Then state separately whether one interview benefited from stronger role fit, easier questions, or more favorable interviewer signals.

When the user asks “only score,” return the execution score first. Add role fit in one short phrase only when it materially explains the result.
